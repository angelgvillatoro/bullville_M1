Judo for BJJ

Takedowns that are useful for starting on the feet in BJJ, how to defend them, and which BJJ techniques come from judo. Knee risk is rated for an operated meniscus: what does the damage is flexion under load combined with twisting. Check it with your coach and with whoever looks after your knee.

Ukemi before throwing. Learn to fall backwards (ushiro), to the side (yoko) and rolling forward (mae mawari): chin to chest, slap the mat with the whole arm, and never post on a straight arm.
